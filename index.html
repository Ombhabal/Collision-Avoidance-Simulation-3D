Sea state sets the wave size and whitecaps; with Ship motion on, every ship (and the bridge / chase camera) heaves, pitches and rolls on the same waves that are drawn. The current moves the sea surface (streaks, foam and waves drift towards the set direction, in step with the simulation clock; strong currents add tide rips). It is a visual effect: speeds in the simulation are over the ground, so CPA, TCPA and COLREG roles are not changed. The moon phase shows at night: it sets the moon in the sky, the silver moon path and glow on the water, and how dark the sea and sky are. Environment is visual only: it never changes ship position, speed, CPA/TCPA or status.

Recommended CPA / TCPA by navigation zone

ZoneCPATCPA
Open ocean / deep sea1.0 to 2.0 nm15 to 30 min
Coastal / moderate traffic0.5 to 1.0 nm10 to 15 min
Harbour / congested waters0.2 to 0.5 nm5 to 10 min

CPA is the smallest distance the target will pass from you if both ships hold course and speed. TCPA is the time until that closest point.

COLLISION: the target is closing and its predicted CPA is below the zone minimum (1.0 nm open ocean, 0.5 nm coastal, 0.2 nm harbour), whatever the TCPA. DANGER: CPA is inside your CPA limit and TCPA is at or below the alarm. CAUTION: CPA is inside the limit but TCPA is still beyond the alarm.

These bands are guidance, not COLREG rules. Master's standing orders and the company safety management system take priority. Large or slow-turning ships should use the upper end of each band.

Help: controls and symbols

Keys: Space start/pause, R reset (new random scenario), N night, 1 Orbit, 2 Chase, 3 Bridge, C centre view, Esc close panels. Drag the sea to rotate and tilt in every view; right-drag or Shift-drag slides the camera over the sea in every view; W A S D move the camera (Shift = fast); wheel zooms (toward the pointer in Orbit); double-click a ship to zoom to it, double-click the sea to fly there; F zooms to the selected ship, 0 fits all ships; the Camera on list puts the camera on any ship (Chase and Bridge then follow that ship); Up / Down = speed and Left / Right = course of your own ship (or camera turn and tilt when the arrow-control box is unticked), + / - zoom, Q / E roll. Click a ship to select it.

CPA = predicted minimum distance between own ship and a target (nm). TCPA = time until that point (min); negative or "opening" means the range is already increasing.

Status: SAFE (CPA above limit), CAUTION (CPA below limit, TCPA beyond alarm), DANGER (below limit, TCPA within alarm), COLLISION (CPA inside collision zone). Colour is always paired with a word or symbol.

Roles: GIVE-WAY, STAND-ON, KEEP CLEAR (target at anchor or aground). The encounter table assumes both vessels are in sight; in restricted visibility Rule 19 applies instead. This is a training aid with simplified rule tests, not a certification of COLREG compliance.

Environment (sunset, fog, reduced effects) is visual only. The simulation models course and speed only, so heading equals course; wind, current and leeway are not modelled.

Anchored, aground or not-making-way vessels are forced to speed 0. Roles are shown in colour only when the target is not SAFE. In the 3D view, the tag next to a target says whether that target is stand-on or give-way (T3 · stand-on means T3 stands on and you give way). The tag next to Own shows YOUR role in the same way (Own · give-way, or Own · give-way T1 · stand-on T3 when the roles differ per target). "Its role" is what the target must do.

Avoiding action log

Drag anywhere on the sea to rotate and tilt the view (left mouse button or one finger; the Drag does setting in the panel switches this to panning). Right-drag or Shift-drag does the other action. Wheel, or two fingers pinching, zooms; twist two fingers to turn. Double-click (double-tap) a ship to zoom to it or the sea to fly there; W A S D or the Move arrows slide the camera over the ocean in every view. Drag a ship to move it, click your own ship to select it. In the chase view a drag circles your ship, in the bridge view it looks around; the wheel zooms in all views. Keys: Space start / pause, R reset, N day and night, 1 to 3 camera (orbit, chase, bridge), C centre on own ship, H hide the panel.
